Tekzite Browser v10.5.87 validation
===================================

Issue fixed
-----------
The Windows DWM pointer fallback samples the global physical mouse. Previously it
used only Tekzite's screen rectangle, so a click on another application covering
the browser could still be injected into Chromium underneath.

Fix
---
- Uses WindowFromPoint to identify the actual native window under the pointer.
- New synthetic Chromium presses require the hit window to be the DWM page host,
  edge_host, or a native child of edge_host.
- Other applications and Tekzite popups/dialogs block the fallback press.
- Drags that genuinely started on the page still receive their matching release.
