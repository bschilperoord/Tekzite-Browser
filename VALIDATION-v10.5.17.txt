Tekzite Browser v10.5.17 validation

Focus: animated menu surfaces and click feedback.

Checks
- Animated popup menus use a borderless Tk toplevel, not native tk.Menu.
- Reveal uses a short alpha + vertical ease-out sequence.
- Command rows provide hover/press feedback and invoke after a 45 ms tactile pulse.
- Cascades support hover/click opening.
- Menu bar buttons stay selected while a popup is open and toggle closed on a second click.
- Outside click and keyboard navigation dismiss/select cleanly.
