Tekzite Browser v10.5.32 validation

Focus: eliminate close-then-new-tab latency when the closed tab had a live Chromium page.

Expected architecture
- Closed target destruction is idle-debounced for 1800 ms.
- New-tab and navigation activity resets that retirement grace.
- Active close handoff waits 180 ms and can be cancelled before activation starts.
- Blank replacement skips Chromium activation.
- DWM hide prefers ShowWindowAsync.
- Last-tab blank replacement is shown immediately without a 250 ms teardown timer.
