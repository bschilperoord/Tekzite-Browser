Tekzite Browser v10.5.32 validation

Focus: prevent the entire Tk application from freezing after closing the active Chromium tab.

Design checks
- Active close keeps the existing DWM/native presentation mounted during replacement activation.
- Tekzite chrome selects the replacement immediately without waiting for Chromium.
- GUI-thread presentation state updates perform zero CDP/session-start I/O.
- Device-metric cleanup is queued on the Chromium worker.
- Closed Chromium target retirement waits until that target is no longer the visible presentation source.
- Native blank canvas explicitly clears _chromium_frame_target_id.
