Tekzite Browser v10.5.7 validation
=================================

DWM interaction hardening release.

Automated coverage verifies:
- single/double/triple clickCount progression and reset after timeout/distance;
- mouseMoved packets use clickCount=0;
- held-left drag motion is coalesced and the final pending move is queued before release;
- both DWM and software surfaces bind middle-click link handling;
- non-link middle clicks are replayed as CDP middle press/release;
- right-click focuses the pointed editable before resolving menu metadata;
- Shift+wheel maps to horizontal delta and forwards CDP modifier bits;
- printable AltGr characters use Input.insertText;
- v10.5.6 DPI/CSS pointer alignment remains covered by the existing regression suite.

Windows-only compositor behavior still requires a real Windows/DWM smoke test.
