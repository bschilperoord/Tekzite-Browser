Tekzite Browser v10.5.32 validation

Focus: immediate new-tab interaction after closing an active tab.

- New-tab creation invalidates any pending tab-switch serial before selecting the new tab.
- Pending activation future is cancelled when possible.

- Existing active-close DWM detach and watchdog behavior remains intact.
