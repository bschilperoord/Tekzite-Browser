Tekzite Browser v10.5.9 validation
=================================

Inline new-tab button release.

Automated coverage verifies:
- the + button is parented to the tab-items row rather than the outer tab bar;
- tab refresh preserves the persistent + button while rebuilding dynamic tabs;
- right placement means immediately after the final visible tab;
- left placement remains supported before the first visible tab;
- the tab-items row still expands, leaving unused strip space to the right without dragging + to the far edge.

A Windows smoke test should confirm the visual spacing with one and multiple tabs.
