Tekzite Browser v10.5.24 validation

Focus: animated new-tab opening.

Checks
- New tabs after the initial tab receive a monotonic animation start timestamp.
- Soft and Classic tab widgets begin at the current animated width and expand to their target width.
- Animation progress survives tab-strip rebuilds because it is stored by tab id.
- The inline new-tab button naturally slides as the new tab expands.
- Quiet Mode or disabled Animations bypass the transition.
