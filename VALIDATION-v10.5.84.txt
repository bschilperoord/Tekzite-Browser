Cloudflare / ChatGPT compatibility hardening
--------------------------------------------
- Chromium no longer launches with --remote-debugging-port=0.
- Tekzite selects an available loopback port and launches Chromium with that explicit port.
- Explicit-port mode keeps the existing loopback URL/port validation and Windows Chromium/profile listener ownership checks.
- DevToolsActivePort parsing remains available for compatibility/recovery.
- Referer stripping now excludes only chatgpt.com, openai.com, and challenges.cloudflare.com.
- x-client-data and x-chrome-connected stripping remain global, including on those compatibility hosts.
- Ad/tracker blocking, HTTPS-first, DNT/GPC, third-party-cookie controls, QUIC/DoH restrictions, and other Privacy Lockdown protections remain enabled.
