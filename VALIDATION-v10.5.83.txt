Tekzite Browser v10.5.83 validation
Date: 2026-09-26

Issue investigated
------------------
A late Chromium navigation could become visible under the wrong Tekzite tab.
The navigation completion callback used the globally active tab and abandoned
workers as soon as the global navigation generation changed. Chromium could
already have created/activated a target by then, leaving DWM and Tekzite chrome
out of sync. Google-auth completion and DWM taskbar restore also had ownership
paths that relied on the current/global tab rather than the originating/selected
target.

Fix
---
- Navigation completion is bound to owner tab id + page-state epoch.
- Stale/background completions cannot mutate the active tab.
- The selected Chromium target is reactivated after a stale completion.
- Unowned stale replacement targets are retired.
- Google-auth return is bound to its source tab.
- DWM restore explicitly reactivates the selected tab target before attach.
- Linux x86_64 packaging uses a bundled native Tekzite Network helper.
- Linux forces the supported CDP software presentation rather than entering the
  Windows-only DWM attach path.
